Stylised anatomy, not a segmentation of a real person: scalp fat, a three-layer skull (cortical bone around fatty marrow), CSF, a folded cortex with slit-like sulci, white matter, deep gray nuclei, lateral and third ventricles, cerebellum and brainstem. Tissue values are the 3 T values used by the contrast simulator; the skull's cortical bone is treated as signal void. Weighted images are ideal spin-echo signal equations, not a full simulation.
