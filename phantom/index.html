Procedural head phantom

A generated, labeled head and neck with no stored data. For judging the phantom itself; it will feed the contrast simulator later.

Stylised anatomy, not a segmentation of a real person: scalp fat, a three-layer skull (cortical bone around fatty marrow), CSF, a folded cortex with radial sulci, an interhemispheric fissure, temporal lobes, white matter, deep gray nuclei, ventricles, cerebellum and brainstem; and a face with eyes, orbital fat, nose, ears, lips, mandible, zygomatic arches, sinuses and oral cavity, plus a short neck with vertebrae, spinal cord and airway. Tissue values are the contrast simulator's 3.0 T table (so WM and GM share T2 = 80 ms and T2-weighted contrast comes from PD and T2* is not used here), plus illustrative muscle, lesion and bone rows; the skull's cortical bone is treated as signal void. Weighted images are ideal spin-echo signal equations, not a full simulation.
