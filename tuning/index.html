Coil tuning & matching

A loop coil made resonant at the Larmor frequency and matched to a 50 Ω line, and what a patient does to it.

Scanner and coil

Capacitors

The − and + buttons step a capacitor by 0.5%, like turning a trimmer. Changing the field strength, loop or loading keeps the capacitors as they are: the coil on the bench does not change.

Model: a circular loop of 2 mm wire (L = μ₀R[ln(8R/a) − 2]); coil loss from the unloaded Q; patient loss for tissue of conductivity 0.5 S/m, growing with frequency² and loop radius³; the patient also adds a little stray capacitance, pulling the resonance down. B1+ is at the loop centre for 100 W from the amplifier: B1+ is half the linear field, because only one of its two counter-rotating halves turns with the spins.
